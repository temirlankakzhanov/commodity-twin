    Optimal flows

    A linear programme (HiGHS via SciPy) sets the flow on each sea lane to maximise total margin within supply, demand and chokepoint capacity.

    Volumes, freight and capacities are assumptions.

        VaR/ES backtest · verdicts

        Each model forecasts VaR and ES day by day on the plan's own book. Kupiec and conditional-coverage tests, the Basel traffic light and an ES Z2 test decide PASS, REVIEW or FAIL.

        Exception: a day with loss above VaR. ES Z2 above zero means ES is too low.
